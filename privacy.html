Privacy policy

Vizion Onvideo · last updated 13 October 2026

Who uses this app

Vizion Onvideo is a private tool. Only its owner uses it, to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. There are no other users and no public sign-up.

Which Google data it uses, and why

  • YouTube (youtube.force-ssl): to upload videos the owner has approved, set their thumbnail and captions, and read the list of the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view and watch-time statistics of the owner's own channel.

The app does not read anything else from the Google account: no e-mail, no contacts, no files.

Where the data is stored

The access token from Google and the channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

How to revoke access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app then stops working for that channel until it is connected again.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
