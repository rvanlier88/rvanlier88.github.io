Privacy policy

Vizion Onvideo · last updated 13 October 2026

Who uses this app

Vizion Onvideo is a private tool. Only its owner uses it, to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and TikTok account. There are no other users and no public sign-up.

Which Google data it uses, and why

  • YouTube (youtube.force-ssl): to upload videos the owner has approved, set their thumbnail and captions, and read the list of the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view and watch-time statistics of the owner's own channel.

The app does not read anything else from the Google account: no e-mail, no contacts, no files.

Which TikTok data it uses, and why

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): the display name and account ID, so the dashboard can show which TikTok account is connected.
  • Video upload (video.upload): to send the owner's own videos to the drafts (inbox) of that TikTok account. The owner adds the caption and posts each video from the TikTok app.

The app does not read followers, messages, likes, comments or any other TikTok data, and it does not post anything publicly by itself.

Where the data is stored

The access tokens from Google and TikTok, the connected account name and the channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

How to revoke access

  • Google: at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (or "Manage app permissions"), or with the disconnect button (ONTKOPPELEN) in the dashboard, which deletes the token from the server.

The app then stops working for that account until it is connected again.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Nederlands. Vizion Onvideo is een eigen hulpmiddel waarmee alleen de eigenaar video's op de eigen YouTube-kanalen zet, korte video's als concept naar het eigen TikTok-account stuurt en de cijfers van die kanalen leest. Van TikTok gebruikt het alleen de accountnaam en het recht om video's in de concepten te zetten. De tokens van Google en TikTok en de statistieken staan alleen op de eigen server en worden met niemand gedeeld. Toegang intrekken kan altijd: bij Google via myaccount.google.com/permissions, bij TikTok in de app onder Instellingen en privacy → Beveiliging → Apps en services.
