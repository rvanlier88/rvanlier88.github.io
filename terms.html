Terms of service

Vizion Onvideo · last updated 13 October 2026

What this is

Vizion Onvideo is a private tool that runs on its owner's own server. It makes videos from the owner's own material and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channels, and sends short videos as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to anyone else: there is no sign-up, no account for other people and no paid service.

Use

  • Only the owner connects accounts to the app, and only accounts that belong to the owner.
  • Only content the owner has the rights to is published: the owner's own music, artwork and footage, or material under a licence that allows it.
  • Every video is published in line with the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and their community guidelines. AI-generated or AI-assisted content is labelled where these platforms require it.
  • On TikTok, the app only places videos in the drafts of the connected account. Posting, the caption and the privacy settings are chosen by the owner in the TikTok app.

Third-party services

The app uses the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and the TikTok Content Posting API. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy and the TikTok Terms of Service. How data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, without any warranty. Access can be revoked at any time, as described in the privacy policy, after which the app stops working for that account.
